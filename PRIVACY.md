# Privacy Policy — LensWhisper Object Explorer

Last updated: 6 October 2026

**Short version: this app collects no data at all.** It has no internet permission, so nothing it
sees — not your photos, not what it finds in them — can leave your phone. There are no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the app does with your photos

- **Photos you take with the app's camera** are analysed on your phone to find the objects in them,
  held only in the phone's memory while that happens, and then discarded. For Explore Photo by Touch,
  the photo is written to the app's private cache (which other apps cannot read) while the Explore
  screen is open, and is overwritten by the next one.
- **Photos other apps send to it** (for example LensWhisper, through the API described in
  [api/API.md](api/API.md)) are read into memory, analysed, and discarded straight away. They are
  never written to storage and never logged. The result goes back only to the app that sent the photo.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to take the photo you ask it to analyse. Nothing is recorded in the background.
- **Vibration** — a short tick when your finger crosses onto a different object in Explore.

The app does **not** have the internet permission, and therefore cannot send anything anywhere. You
can check this in Android's app info for the app.

## Your rights

Because the app collects and stores no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct,
delete or hand over. You still have the rights given by the GDPR (Art. 15–21) and the right to
complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

## Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. It collects no data from anyone, including young users.

Any change to this policy will be published in this file and will come with an app update.
