LensWhisper Object Explorer finds the objects in a photo — what they are, their main colours and their exact outlines — and tells you through TalkBack. It is made for blind and low-vision people, and everything happens on your own phone.

THREE SIMPLE BUTTONS
• Detect Objects — names the objects in front of the camera.
• Identify Objects and Colors — names each object and its main colours, for example "Cup, red and white".
• Explore Photo by Touch — the photo fills the screen. Move your finger over it and hear the name and colours of the object under your finger, with a short vibration each time you cross onto a different one. "List all objects" reads them all.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
The app has no internet permission. Nothing it sees can leave your phone: no uploads, no accounts, no ads, no analytics.

WORKS WITH LENSWHISPER
If you also use LensWhisper, it uses Object Explorer automatically for Identify Objects and Colors, Explore Photo by Touch, Describe TV Image and Explore and Describe, for more accurate object outlines.

FOR DEVELOPERS
Any Android app can send Object Explorer a photo and get back each object's name, confidence, box and pixel mask, through a documented, open API. The source code and the API guide are on GitHub: github.com/appricodes/LensWhisper-Segmenter

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Object Explorer is free software under the GNU AGPL v3. Object detection uses the YOLOv8 segmentation model by Ultralytics and recognises 80 common kinds of object: people, animals, vehicles, furniture, food, kitchenware, electronics and more.

IMPORTANT
Results are AI guesses and can be wrong. Never rely on them for safety, health, medicine, money, or for moving around.
